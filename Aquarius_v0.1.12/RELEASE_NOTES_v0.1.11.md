# Aquarius v0.1.11 — Showcase & Release Testing

- Hidden showcase/demo mode covering resident life, feeding, time-of-day, weather, visitors and calendar events.
- Enter from **Settings > About** by holding the **Firmware** row for approximately four seconds.
- Tap PREV/NEXT to browse, tap AUTO to pause/resume, and hold the screen for roughly two seconds to exit.
- Demo mode uses temporary simulated readings and does not overwrite settings, event timers or live weather data.
