# Aquarius Changelog

## v0.1.3 — Weather Ecology

- Locked the established v0.1.2 dashboard layout.
- Added a persistent Weather Ecology state inside `AquariumEngine`.
- Weather conditions now transition gradually instead of snapping between modes.
- Added Open-Meteo sunrise/sunset driven twilight lighting.
- Added live wind influence to the water surface and plant sway.
- Clear weather raises fish activity and bubble rate.
- Cloud/overcast progressively mutes aquarium activity and lighting.
- Rain now increases surface movement, creates splashes/rainfall and moves fish deeper.
- Thunderstorms add stronger water movement, deeper fish behaviour and brief multi-flash lightning.
- Snow and snow showers add drifting particles and calmer/deeper behaviour.
- Fog adds a subtle moving haze layer and reduced fish activity.
- Night mode now includes a restrained bioluminescent water effect.
- Retained v0.1.2 fish personalities and feeding state machine.
- Retained touchscreen feeding, squid, octopus, rare visitors and submarine.
- Updated boot/version text to v0.1.3.

## v0.1.2 — Behaviour Pass

- Added individual fish personalities: Calm, Explorer, Social and Darting.
- Added smoother direction and speed changes.
- Added independent depth targets and voluntary turns.
- Reworked feeding into approach, frenzy and recovery stages.
- Improved squid pulsed propulsion and octopus crawl/pause behaviour.
- Improved jellyfish motion.

## v0.1.1

- Moved fish feeding from BOOT button to touchscreen tap.
- Added tap-position feeding.
- Added squid and octopus rare visitors.

## v0.1.0

- Introduced the expanded Aquarium Engine architecture.
- Moved indoor pressure beneath humidity in the INDOOR panel.
